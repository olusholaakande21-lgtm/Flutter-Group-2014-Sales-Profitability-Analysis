# Flutter Group: 2014 Sales & Profitability Analysis.

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI sales and profitability dashboard** built from **369 US sales transactions generating $435,066 in 2014 revenue**.

The objective was to transform raw transaction-level data into an interactive management dashboard that provides insights into **sales performance, product performance, regional performance, customer concentration, seasonality, salesperson performance, and payment preferences** to support 2015 business planning.

The project follows a structured analytical workflow from raw data preparation through visualization, insight generation, and business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer key management questions:

1. Which salespeople are performing best, and where is additional support required?
2. Which cities and regions contribute the most revenue?
3. Which products and categories generate the highest revenue?
4. How does sales performance change throughout the year?
5. How concentrated is revenue among key customers and ship names?
6. What payment methods do customers prefer?
7. Where are the strongest opportunities for revenue growth?

---

## 📊 Dataset

* **Dataset:** 2014 sales transaction data
* **Transactions:** 369
* **Revenue:** $435,066
* **Geographic coverage:** 12 US states
* **Shipping cities:** 9
* **Granularity:** Order-level transaction data

The dataset contains information relating to customers, salespeople, products, categories, regions, shipping locations, orders, and payment methods.

# 📈 Key Findings

### Geography

**New York** generated the highest shipping-city revenue at approximately **$67,180**.

Regional revenue was relatively balanced:

* North — **32.6% ($141.7K)**
* East — **24.9% ($108.3K)**
* South — **21.6% ($93.9K)**
* West — **21.0% ($91.3K)**

Although the West had active cities, it generated the lowest regional revenue, indicating potential for targeted growth.

### Product Performance

**Beverages** generated the highest category revenue at approximately **$110,577**, followed by Sauces at approximately **$69,000**.

The analysis also demonstrated that **sales volume does not always translate into revenue**.

For example:

* Coffee — 1,641 units / $75,486
* Curry Sauce — 1,725 units / $69,000
* Green Tea — 1,589 units / only $4,751

This highlights potential pricing and product-mix opportunities.

### Salesperson Performance

**Nancy Freehafer ($104,252)** and **Anne Larsen ($93,858)** were the strongest performers, together accounting for nearly half of total sales.

**Jan Kotas ($16,350)** recorded the lowest contribution, highlighting a potential need for coaching, territory review, or additional sales support.

### Seasonality

Sales showed a clear seasonal pattern.

Performance strengthened toward the middle and end of the year, with **December recording the highest monthly revenue at approximately $66.6K from 57 orders**.

The early-year period represented the most consistent weakness and presents an opportunity for targeted sales campaigns.

### Customer Concentration

Revenue was concentrated among a relatively small group of customers.

The top five ship names generated approximately **$235,369**, representing about **54% of total 2014 revenue**.

This creates a potential dependency risk and highlights the importance of developing mid-sized accounts.

### Payment Methods

**Credit card payments accounted for approximately 39.6% of revenue ($172K)**, making it the dominant payment channel.

This indicates the importance of maintaining a reliable and seamless card-payment experience.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Invest in the West region** while protecting strong performance in the North and East.
2. **Address the Q1 and early-Q2 sales slowdown** through targeted promotions and customer outreach.
3. **Reduce customer concentration risk** by developing mid-sized and emerging accounts.
4. **Prioritize high-value products** while reviewing pricing and profitability for high-volume, low-revenue products.
5. **Support lower-performing salespeople** through coaching, territory reviews, and replication of successful sales practices.
6. **Maintain reliable credit-card payment infrastructure**, given its contribution to overall revenue.
